## Design
### Seasons and ranks
- Season = one calendar month **in UTC** (`seasonKeyNow()` = `YYYY-MM` of the UTC date). The rules can only check the
  server clock, so the season key must be the UTC month; in India the season turns over at 05:30 on the 1st. The
  first season is `RANK_FROM = '2026-11'`: no rank history, season rewards or first-time items before it.
- Counted minutes = completed focus blocks (the session log, Finish-early blocks included) grouped by the UTC day the
  block ended, each UTC day capped at **14 h (840 min)**, summed over the season (`seasonTotals()`).
- Thresholds (hours) as in the brief: Bronze 0, Silver 10, Gold 25, Platinum 50, Diamond 80, Master 120,
  Grandmaster 180. Bronze–Diamond have three divisions (III → II → I) splitting the range evenly; minutes
  `RANK_T = [0, 200, 400, 600, 900, 1200, 1500, 2000, 2500, 3000, 3600, 4200, 4800, 5600, 6400, 7200, 10800]`
  → index 0 (Bronze III) … 14 (Diamond I), 15 Master, 16 Grandmaster. Kept the brief's numbers: at 3 h a day a student
  reaches Diamond (80 h) around day 27; at 6 h a day Grandmaster (180 h) on day 30 — Grandmaster stays rare and every
  serious student has a reachable next step each week.
- Soft reset (placement floor) from last season's peak: Gold → Bronze I, Platinum → Silver III, Diamond → Silver I,
  Master → Gold III, Grandmaster → Gold II; Bronze / Silver → none. Shown rank = max(rank from hours, floor). The floor
  only lifts the badge; the leaderboard sorts by counted hours.
- Season reward (peak rank of a finished season, claimed once in the next season): Bronze 10 · Silver 40 · Gold 80 ·
  Platinum 150 · Diamond 250 · Master 400 · Grandmaster 600 coins + a season badge on the trophy shelf. Claims
  `S.game.claims.season['YYYY-MM:<peak>']`; the coin ledger pays one claim per season — the highest peak claimed by
  then (two devices can claim different peaks; merged claims never pay twice).
- First time ever reaching a rank (earned hours, not the floor): Silver frame, Gold frame, Platinum aura, Diamond aura,
  Master poster (Pomeo's room), Grandmaster animated frame. Grants with `src: 'rank:<major>'`.
- Rank-up: `S.game.claims.rkup['YYYY-MM:<idx>']` (add-only; the floor is the season's starting point, not a rank-up).
  During a focus block: a small toast now and the celebration (reward reveal) held until the block ends; after: the
  celebration; first load: a notice.
### Published fields (all optional)
- **`rankStats/{uid}` (new collection)**: the look for board rows (`username`, `level`, `tier`, `frame`, `color`,
  `avatar`, `aura`), `global`, `seasonKey`, `seasonMin`, `prevPeak`, `streak`, `bestStreak`, `streakDay` (last day the
  goal was met — the viewer's check for a live streak), `joined` (account creation, from Firebase Auth metadata).
  First tried as extra fields on `publicStats`: that document's update rule then ran out of the rules engine's
  1,000-expression budget per request (the error also left the emulator refusing everything until restarted), so the
  numbers got a small document of their own. `publicStats` is back to its v1.18 fields; its rule now computes the time
  allowance once and checks only the changed keys on update (both cheaper, same meaning).
- `publicProfiles`: unchanged. (A first version also put the season there; review: the profile's rules can't bound it
  as tightly, so badges now read `rankStats` only — fetched with friends' profiles and for people shown on screen.)
- Rules (`rankStats`): owner-only writes; season key = the current UTC month; `seasonMin` ≤ 840 × UTC day of month;
  grows only within the season by at most the real time since the last write + 4 h; `prevPeak` 0 on create, fixed
  within a season, at a season change ≤ the rank of the minutes the document held; a new document starts at ≤ 240 min
  (deleting and re-creating it can't jump). Streak: whole numbers, `bestStreak ≥ streak`, `bestStreak` ≤ days since
  `joined` + 2, `joined` never changes, the best never goes down and grows by at most the elapsed days + 2 per write. Reads like `publicStats` (owner, friends, or global).
- Indexes: `rankStats` (global ↑, seasonKey ↑, seasonMin ↓), (global ↑, streakDay ↑, streak ↓), (global ↑, bestStreak ↓).
  The global Current board asks only for live streaks (`streakDay in` the last 3 days + tomorrow), so dead streaks
  can't fill its top 50.
### Where it shows
- `personLook` gains `rank`; `lookName` draws the badge (friend rows, leaderboard rows and podium, session tables and
  lists, clan members, challenge cards). Hidden in True Minimalist.
- Timer: a compact line under the next streak milestone; profile: a Rank card (progress, season end, placement, last
  season, first-time rewards); trophy shelf: season badges.
- Leaderboard: kinds Focus time · Garden · **Ranks** (this season) · **Streaks** (Current / Best), Friends / Global /
  Clans as before. Streak rows show the best milestone flame; a stale streak (last goal day older than 2 days before
  the viewer's today) counts as 0 and is left off the Current board.
