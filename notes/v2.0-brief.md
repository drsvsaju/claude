## 1. Research summary (why these features)
I researched other focus apps and the psychology behind them. Use this to guide design decisions; don't copy any app's branding or art.

- **Accountability and body doubling.** Sharing a goal raises follow-through, and doing it in a committed accountability session raises it further (cited figures: 65% → 95%, ASTD). Virtual coworking apps (Flow Club, Caveday, Flown, Focusmate) work through scheduled sessions with **a short goal-setting moment at the start and a debrief at the end**. A 2025 study found people with ADHD worked faster and reported better focus with a body double present, and **shared progress bars** were the most liked element.
- **Fresh start effect** (Dai, Milkman & Riis): motivation spikes at temporal landmarks (new week, new month, new term, after holidays) and then fades quickly, so a fresh-start prompt works best with a **follow-up a few days later** and a reminder of past progress.
- **Healthy streaks.** Long streaks start to feel stressful around 30+ days, and breaking a long streak tends to make people quit rather than restart (Silverman & Barasch, 2023). Good design plans for recovery from day one: **earned (never bought) freezes, rest days, a short recovery quest to repair a broken streak, a pause mode, and "graduation" into permanent unlocks** so the counter matters less over time. Finch's pause mode is a good example of non-punishing design.
- **Friends.** Duolingo's **Friend Streak** (shared streak with up to 5 friends) made learners 22% more likely to do their daily lesson, and their biggest bottleneck was **sending the first invite**. **Friends Quests** pair two friends each week on a shared goal for about 5 days with a big reward.
- **Commitment devices.** Forest kills your tree if you leave the app mid-session, and "Plant Together" makes a group share that consequence.
- **Subject tracking and comparison.** YPT (very popular with NEET/JEE students) tracks time per subject, shows a calendar whose colour deepens as you study more, real-time rankings within your category, group status, and **relative stats** ("compared with people like you").
- **Referrals that work** are **double-sided**, give **rewards that are part of the product** (Dropbox gave storage), use **milestones and caps**, and use **deep links**. Finch rewards referrals with exclusive pets. Referred users retain better than users from ads.
- **Avoid:** making rewards the only reason to study (overjustification), anxiety-driven streak pressure, and judgemental messages.

## PHASE 6 — Psychology-backed focus features
Implement the ones you can make solid (aim for most of this list), and list the rest as proposals in the report. Everything is optional, skippable and never interrupts a running focus block.
1. **Session intention and debrief.** Before starting, an optional one-line private intention ("What will done look like?"). After the block, a 5-second debrief: did you do it (yes / partly / no) and a 1–5 focus rating. Both are stored privately, shown in history, and feed Insights. One tap to skip; a setting to turn it off.
2. **When-and-where plans** (implementation intentions): "I'll study ⟨subject⟩ at ⟨time⟩ at ⟨place⟩". Creates a planned block on the calendar plus a reminder, and Pomeo reminds you of the plan when it's due.
3. **Fresh starts.** On a new week, a new month and the start of each event, show a "Fresh start" card: last period's recap, one slightly bigger goal to pick, and a follow-up nudge 2–3 days later. Don't use birthdays (we don't store dates of birth).
4. **Healthier streaks:**
   - **Rest day:** pick one weekly rest day that doesn't break the streak (optional).
   - **Pause mode:** up to 7 days for exams, travel or illness, keeping the count (limited per month).
   - **Earned freezes:** freezes are earned through focus, never bought with real money (check how the existing freezes work and keep them compatible).
   - **Streak repair:** if a streak breaks, a 48-hour recovery quest (e.g. focus 2× your daily goal) restores it, at most once a month.
   - **Graduation:** after 100 days, a permanent badge and gentler copy, so the counter carries less pressure.
   - Rewrite streak copy to be encouraging, never shaming.
5. **Friend Streaks:** a shared streak with up to 5 friends. It grows on days when both of you hit at least a minimum (e.g. 25 minutes). Shown on friend rows, profiles and Posts. Invites and accepts go through the inbox. Includes rules, anti-cheat and cost limits.
6. **Buddy Quest** (weekly): pick a friend (or get matched with an active friend) for a shared weekly goal, e.g. 10 hours combined in 5 days, with a big reward for both. Includes preset nudges, a shared progress bar (the most liked element in the body-doubling research) and opt-out.
7. **Strict mode** (optional commitment device, Forest-style): if you leave the tab for more than ~15 seconds during a focus block, that block's "focus sprout" wilts. The minutes still count, but you lose the block's bonus (e.g. extra plant XP and a "perfect focus" mark). Warn before it wilts, explain the limits honestly (phone screen locks look like leaving, so offer a short grace and a "screen locked" allowance), and keep it off by default. In Focus together sessions, add an optional table **Pact**: if everyone keeps strict mode, the table earns a shared bonus.
8. **Insights:** best time of day, best block length, subject balance against the weekly plan, focus rating trends, interruptions, overtime habits. Include **relative stats** that only use data you already publish ("this week you focused more than 70% of your friends"), plus a weekly insight card.
9. **Ambient sound mixer:** generated sounds only (WebAudio, nothing copyrighted): rain, café murmur, library, white/brown noise, fireplace, forest. Per-preset defaults, smooth fades, and it respects quiet-during-focus settings.
10. **Resume card:** "Pick up where you left off": the last unfinished intention or subtask, ready to start in one tap.
11. **Distraction tally:** a one-tap "I felt distracted" counter during focus (no pop-up). It shows in the debrief and Insights, and pairs with the existing parking lot.
12. **Break activities:** short guided breathing, stretch cards, the 20-20-20 eye break and a water nudge. Offered in breaks only, alongside flashcards.
13. **JEE/NEET extras:** a **mistake notebook** (log a question, topic, why you got it wrong, and when to retry; this links to flashcards) and a **mock test tracker** (score, subject-wise marks, time, trend chart, and topics to revise). Both are private, synced and exportable.

Each feature needs tests, demo data and a short entry in What's new.

## PHASE 7 — Growth: invite rewards and sharing
Goal: make people **want** to invite friends, honestly, without spam.

### 7a. Personal invite link and rewards
- Every user gets a personal invite link (e.g. `?ref=<username>`). Session, clan and post share links also carry the ref.
- **Double-sided rewards, unlocked on activation:** when an invited friend signs up and **activates** (for example, 3 hours of focus across at least 3 different days within 14 days), the friend gets a welcome gift (coins, a starter seed, a cosmetic) and you get a reward. Activation stops fake accounts from farming rewards.
- **Milestones:** 1 / 3 / 5 / 10 / 25 activated friends unlock **invite-only cosmetics** that can't be earned any other way, such as an aura, frame, pet, Pomeo outfit, room item, an invite-only plant species, and titles like "Study Buddy" and "Ambassador". Show "Brought N friends" on the profile.
- **Auto-connect:** the new user becomes friends with the inviter automatically (a clear checkbox at sign-up, on by default).
- **Buddy boost:** for the first 14 days, studying in the same session as your inviter or invitee gives both of you bonus Pass XP (capped).
- **Clan invites:** when someone joins a clan through a clan link and activates, the clan earns Clan Points.
- **Invite page:** a section (from Friends and Profile) showing your link, share buttons (Web Share, copy, WhatsApp, Discord), joined vs activated friends, progress to the next milestone, and the rewards.
- **Security:** design the Firestore side so invites can't be faked:
  - a referral record is created once by the new user at sign-up, only for a genuinely new account, never self-referral, and the inviter must exist;
  - activation is checked against data the rules can verify (e.g. the invitee's published focus totals);
  - each reward is claimed once per invitee, with sensible caps;
  - write an attack suite.
- Logged-out visitors can't read profiles under the current rules, so a ref landing should greet them using the username in the link ("@siverieg invited you to study together") without reading Firestore.

### 7b. Shareable moments
- Generate good-looking **share cards** as PNGs (story 1080×1920 and square 1080×1080): weekly recap, rank up, streak milestone, Study Pass complete, garden snapshot, boss defeated. Each shows a small QR code / short link with the user's ref.
- Offer sharing **at the right moments** (after a milestone, never during focus), with a "don't ask again" option.

### 7c. Link previews and SEO
- Add proper **Open Graph and Twitter meta tags** and a designed **1200×630 preview image**, so links pasted in Discord, WhatsApp and Instagram look great.
- Add a full favicon set and apple-touch-icon.
- Improve the landing page's title, description and headings for search.
- **Do NOT build a PWA in this release** (no web app manifest, no service worker, no install prompt). That's planned for the next update. Don't add anything that caches `index.html`.
