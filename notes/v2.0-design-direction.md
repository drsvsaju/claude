This is the document to build from. It finishes the direction the panel chose (draft A, "Soft depth"), with the judges' twelve problems fixed and twelve ideas grafted from drafts B and C.

## 1. Direction in five lines

1. **Shape says what a thing is.** A pill is pressed. A square, sunken well is typed in or holds a choice. A rounded sheet is a surface.
2. **Depth separates surfaces; an edge identifies controls.** Cards differ by tone, a 1px top highlight and a soft tinted shadow, and carry no border. Every control carries one 1px edge that measures 3:1 or better on every layer.
3. **One thing leads each screen.** One hero surface, one filled accent button. Supporting cards are a half-step quieter and have no shadow.
4. **The accent is a thin line and one fill, never a glow.** It marks the ring arc, progress, focus and selection. Nothing radiates, so three hours in front of it stays calm.
5. **Every colour is mixed in CSS from the nine palette variables** and measured at WCAG AA in all four test modes. Pomeo, the garden and one leaf on the ring keep it Pomodorolist.

### Notes for pasting

- **Names.** Spacing is `--space-*`, type is `--fs-*`, motion is `--d-1/2/3`, elevation is `--e-1…4`, as the in-progress block already has them. Draft A used `--sp-2`, `--sp-4`, `--sp-6` for 2, 4 and 6px; those names already exist in the app as the v1.7 aliases for 8, 16 and 24px (`--sp-1…6`), so pasting A's block would have silently changed every old layout. The v1.7 aliases (`--sp-1…6`, `--r-sm/md/lg`, `--fs-xs…xl`, `--sh-1/2`, `--radius`) stay until each screen is migrated.
- **Delete the literal `--danger` and `--warn`** from the three base palette blocks at the top of `index.html` (`:root`, `:root[data-theme="dark"]` and the `prefers-color-scheme` copy). They are now derived, and the dark block would otherwise win over the derived value by specificity.
- **Dark dials live in two places**, as the palette already does: `:root[data-theme="dark"]` and `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark){ :root:not([data-theme="light"]) }`. Both are in the block.
- **`--on-accent` is the ninth palette variable.** Each palette supplies it. `--away` reads it to decide whether accent fills are pushed darker (white text) or lighter (dark text), which is what brings white-on-accent from 2.5:1 in v1.20 to 4.99:1 or better.
- **`deriveTokens()` is no longer needed for colour roles.** Borders, focus ring, accent text and text-on-accent all come from the block. Keep the function only as a guard: it can fill `--on-accent` for a palette that lacks one and warn in the console when a palette falls below a bar.
- **`--layer`.** Every surface class sets `--layer` to its own fill. A neutral pad reads it to compute its raised fill, and falls back to `--surface` when unset. Nothing else depends on it.
- **Status hues** take the accent's own lightness and chroma and turn them to a fixed hue (27 red, 75 amber, 150 green). The three hue angles are the only constants in the colour system; they are the same for every palette.
- **Browser floor.** `color-mix()`: Chrome 111, Safari 16.2, Firefox 113 (the app already relies on it). Relative colour (`oklch(from …)`): Chrome 119, Safari 16.4, Firefox 128; older engines take the `@supports not` fallback, which is keyed to the mode. `:has()` is used once (keyboard focus on a well) and degrades to the thicker edge alone.
- `black` and `white` appear only as mixing ends and as the top highlight. They are not palette colours.

Reading the table:

- **Tightest text pair: 4.74:1**, secondary text on a hovered row over the frame in sunset light. The frame is the darkest layer in light mode, so anything tinted on top of it is where a new palette will fail first.
- **Tightest boundary: 3.28:1**, the control edge on the frame in default light (3.26 under Glossed Full). `--k-edge` is the one dial to raise if a palette falls short.
- Draft A's secondary pads measured 1.1–1.4:1 against their card. They now carry the same edge as wells: 3.28 or better everywhere.
- For comparison, v1.20: white on the accent was 2.5:1 in dark, and `--line` on `--surface` about 1.3:1.
- **Exempt by WCAG and left low on purpose:** disabled controls (`--ink-off`, `--hair` edge), and decoration that carries no information alone (the empty track, tick marks, dividers, the step between surfaces).
- **Only two of the 24 palettes are measured.** Before Phase 3 ships, add the other 22 to the `P` table in `proto.html` (or point the script at the app) and run `contrast.mjs` over all 48 modes. Expect a few light palettes with a tinted, mid-tone `--bg` to need `--k-frame` lowered or their `--muted` darkened.

## 4. Component specs

Common to every control:

- **States.** Default, hover, pressed, keyboard focus, disabled. Hover never uses the accent. Focus is always the 2px `--focus` ring with a 2px gap.
- **Selected** is always at least two cues that are not colour: a check or filled shape, plus weight or an outline. The accent tint comes on top.
- **Phone.** Every tap target is 44px or more in both directions. A visual pad may be 36px if its hit box is 44px. Anything that opens a popover on desktop opens a bottom sheet on the phone (4.22).
- Numbers below are CSS pixels. "Pad" means the neutral button recipe in section 2; "well" means the field recipe.

### 4.1 Preset combobox (sheet 01)

- **Trigger:** a well, 40 high. Colour dot 10 · preset name 14/500 · rhythm chip pushed right · up-down chevron 16. Open: the edge turns to `--focus` and thickens to 2px.

### 4.13 Buttons and chips (sheet 13)

- **All buttons are pills.** Heights 32 / 40 / 52; padding 12 / 16 / 32; text 13 / 14 / 17 at 600; icon 16 (18 in large), 8px gap. Icon-only buttons are circles of the same height.

### 4.14 Tooltip and menu (sheet 14)

### 4.18 Toasts (sheet 18)

## 5. Layout rules

**Page grid (desktop, 1180 and up).** A frame holds everything: top bar 60 high and sidebar 232 wide, both on `--frame`. The stage is a `--bg` sheet with a 22px top-left corner and padding 20 / 32 / 24. Content is centred with a 1140 max width. Screens with a lead use two columns, 1.6fr and 1fr, 20px apart; cards in a column are 16px apart.

**Phone (under 600).** No frame: top bar 56 on the stage, 16px side margins, one column, 12px between cards, tab bar 64 plus the safe area. Tablet widths keep the existing slide-in menu and the phone's single column with 24px margins (not drawn).

**Card padding.** 20 on every side. The hero card uses 24. A card whose last element is a row list uses 8 at the bottom, because the last row already holds its own air under the text (a row is at least 44 high around a 19px line); the gap under the last line then reads the same as the gap above the title. (This is the fix for the Today card's row sitting on the edge.)

**Card head.** Title left in 14/600 `--ink`, sentence case. One piece of meta right in 12 `--ink-2` (a date, a count, or a link with an arrow). 12px below it. No icons in front of titles.

**Inside a card.** 12px between related blocks, 16px before a new kind of block, dividers only between rows of the same kind. Never a card inside a card: use a divider or a tile.

**How one element leads.**

| Level | Surface | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Lead | `.card--hero`: brighter layer in dark, larger shadow in light, radius 22, the only place with the accent wash | one per screen: the timer, the main chart on Stats, the card on Profile |
| Working | `.card`: `--surface`, `--e-1` | where the user types or acts next (Session) |
| Supporting | `.card--quiet`: half-step fill, rim only, no shadow | glanceable context (Today, garden, tip) |

- One primary button per view, and it lives on the lead. Screens with no natural action (Stats, Profile) lead with a number in 30/600 or the main chart on the hero card instead.
- Key numbers are 24/600 tabular with a 13 unit and a 12 caption. At most two side by side, split by a vertical divider.
- **Label-left, value-right rows** ("ledger") carry the rest: icon 16, label `--ink-2`, value `--ink` 600 right-aligned, chevron 14; 44 min height; a divider above each row. The Today card is two numbers, one bar with its caption, then five such rows.
- The frame stays quiet: no accent in the top bar, and only the 3px bar and one icon in the sidebar.

**The Timer screen.** Left: the timer (lead) above the Session card (working). Right: Today, garden, tip (all supporting). The event line ("Monsoon Study · 16 days left · 3 / 8 quests · rewards ready") is one line of text at the right of the greeting, not a banner card. The exam countdown is a row in Today. The weekly chart is not on this screen; it belongs to Stats.

**Garden glance.** Seven tiles in one row. Each tile: plant glyph 36, a 3px growth bar, the plant's name (12/500) and its percentage or "Ready" (11). The ready tile adds an accent edge, a tint and a corner check; the footer repeats it in words ("Ready · Basil is ready to harvest") with one small Harvest pad. The card is as tall as its content.

## 6. Motion

Three durations and three curves, from the token block: `--d-1` 120ms, `--d-2` 200ms, `--d-3` 320ms; `--ease-out` for anything arriving or responding, `--ease-in` for anything leaving, `--ease-spring` only for a knob or thumb settling. Animate `transform` and `opacity`; colour may fade; nothing animates layout.

| Change | Full | Reduced | Off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hover on a pad or row | fill and edge fade 120 ease-out; pads lift 1px | fade only | instant |
| Press | 1px down and inner shadow, 120 | shadow only | instant |
| Focus ring | instant | instant | instant |
| Switch | track colour 200 ease-out; knob slides 200 with the spring | colour fades 120, knob jumps | instant |
| Checkbox, radio, chosen segment, chip on | fill and mark fade 120 | same | instant |
| Popover, menu, select | in: 200 ease-out, opacity plus 4px rise and scale from .98, origin at the trigger; out: 120 ease-in, opacity | opacity 120 in and out | instant |
| Tooltip | 120 fade after a 400 delay; no delay between neighbours | same | no fade |
| Dialog | scrim fades 200; panel 320 ease-out, opacity plus 12px rise and scale from .97; out 200 ease-in | opacity 200 | instant |
| Bottom sheet | slides up 320 ease-out; follows the finger while dragged; settles or leaves in 200 | opacity 200, no slide | instant |
| Toast | in: 200 ease-out, 8px rise plus opacity; the stack reflows 200; out 120 ease-in; the life line shrinks linearly with `scaleX` | opacity only; life line still runs | no fade; life line is a static full line |
| Timer arc | `stroke-dashoffset` moves linearly, one-second steps joined by a 1s linear transition; refills in 320 on a phase change | same (the app already allows `stroke-dashoffset` under Reduced) | steps once a second |
| Bars and small ring | value changes animate 320 ease-out (`scaleX`, dashoffset) | 120 | instant |
| Skeleton | 1.4s shimmer | static blocks | static blocks |
| Screen change | 200 opacity plus 8px rise | opacity 120 | none |

- Reduced and Off map to the existing `data-motion="reduced"` and `"off"` rules; `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` behaves as Reduced unless the user chose Off.
- Nothing loops while the timer runs except the arc. No pulsing, no breathing glow, no idle animation on Pomeo during a focus block.
- No shadow or filter animates. The ring has no filter at all, so its once-a-second repaint is a single stroke.
- Sounds and haptics are unchanged and follow their own settings.

The prototype implements hover, press, the focus ring, the switch and the 120ms fills on checkbox, radio, segment and star. The rest are specified, not built.

## 7. Glossed Interface and True Minimalist

Neither is drawn in the prototype. The glass numbers below were measured by compositing (section 3); the look still needs a screenshot pass in Phase 3.

### Glossed (frosted glass)

Glass changes how surfaces are filled. It does not change controls, text colours, radii or layout.

| Surface | Lite (default) | Full | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `.card`, `.card--quiet` | layer at 80%, blur 10px, saturate 130% | layer at 64%, blur 22px, saturate 175% | at 64% the control edge measures 3.26 over the accent blob. v1.20's Full fill (55–58%) was measured at 55%: 3.11 for the edge and 5.04 for secondary text. That passes on these two palettes but leaves too little room for the other 22 |
| `.card--hero` | 88% | 72% | the lead stays the most solid sheet |
| `.pop`, `.dialog`, sheet, toast | 94%, blur as above | 94% | they sit over busy content and hold forms |
| Frame (top bar, sidebar) | as v1.20 | as v1.20 | unchanged |
| Wells | opaque `--well` | opaque | a well is pressed into the sheet; its fill and 3:1 lip must not depend on what is behind |
| Pads | opaque, computed from `--layer` | opaque | same reason |

The app's own glass variables stay (`--glass-fill`, `--glass-blur`, `--glass-sat`, `--gb-a`). What changes:

```css
:root[data-glass]        { --blur-scrim:6px;  --k-lamp:0%; --k-hair:16%; }             /* Lite keeps --glass-fill:80%, blur 10px, saturate 130% */
:root[data-glass="full"] { --blur-scrim:12px; --glass-fill:64%; }                      /* was 58% light / 55% dark */
:root[data-glass][data-theme="dark"] { --k-hair:12%; }                                 /* and the prefers-color-scheme copy */
:root[data-glass] .card  { background-color:color-mix(in srgb, var(--layer) var(--glass-fill), transparent);
                           backdrop-filter:blur(var(--glass-blur)) saturate(var(--glass-sat)); box-shadow:var(--hi), var(--rim); }
:root[data-glass] .card--hero { background-color:color-mix(in srgb, var(--layer) calc(var(--glass-fill) + 8%), transparent); box-shadow:var(--e-1); }
:root[data-glass] :is(.pop, .dialog, .bsheet, .toast) { background-color:color-mix(in srgb, var(--layer) 94%, transparent); }
```

- **The rim doubles** (`--k-hair` 16% light, 12% dark) so a translucent sheet keeps an edge; the top highlight stays.
- **Shadows drop one level** (card to rim only, hero to `--e-1`). Blur already separates the sheet from the page, and a soft shadow under glass looks dirty.
- **The lamp wash turns off**; the accent blobs behind the glass (`--gb-a`, 42% light and 30% dark, as today) do that job.
- **The scrim** keeps its tint and adds `backdrop-filter: blur(var(--blur-scrim))`.
- `backdrop-filter` goes on sheets only, never on rows, pads or wells, and never on something that moves every frame. `data-glass-solid` keeps its meaning: 96% fill, no blur.
- Measured worst case, a card over the full-strength blob: secondary text 5.72 (Lite) and 5.26 (Full); accent text 5.43 and 4.98; control edge 3.53 and 3.26. The measurement composites the fills and ignores the `saturate()` boost, which changes the backdrop's chroma far more than its lightness.

### True Minimalist

The setting hides levels, coins, shop, quests, badges and rewards. The direction adapts by removing, not by restyling:

- **Top bar:** search, inbox and the profile pad remain; the coins and rank pads, the level label and the bag go. The search pad may grow into the freed space up to 360.
- **Sidebar:** hidden destinations leave no gap; group labels stay. The active row's bar and the count pills turn neutral (`--ink` bar, as the existing `data-minimal` rule already does), so the accent is left for Start, the arc, focus and selection.
- **Timer card:** the ring cap becomes a plain dot (no leaf), the wash is off (`--k-lamp:0%`), Pomeo follows "Show Pomeo". Quick start, Reset and Skip stay.
- **Right column:** the event line, the garden card and the tip go. The Today card keeps its two numbers, bar and the rows that still apply (reminder, to-dos, plan, exam). It stops stretching: content height, 48px rows, top-aligned, with the page below it left empty rather than filled.
- **Surfaces:** supporting cards are already shadowless; working cards drop from `--e-1` to the rim, and the hero from `--e-2` to `--e-1`. Layers, radii, wells and pads are unchanged, so every control still passes the same contrast table.
- **Toasts and empty states** lose reward lines ("+12 coins") and, with Pomeo off, use an icon tile.
- **With Glossed on,** keep the existing softer values (`--gb-a:16%`, fill 82%, blur 8px).

## 8. Do / Don't

**Do**

- Derive every new colour from the tokens. If a role is missing, add a formula to the block and a pair to `contrast.mjs`.
- Give every control the 1px `--edge`. Tell wells from pads by shape and light: square and sunken, or pill and raised.
- Keep one filled accent button and one hero surface per view.
- Put a number or a word next to every dot, bar and badge.
- Mark selection with a check or an outline plus weight; add the accent tint on top.
- End every value popover with the same footer: shortcut, Clear, Done.
- Use label-left, value-right rows for facts, and 24px numerals for the two that matter most.
- Keep phone targets at 44 and test them with `shoot.mjs`.
- Write sentence case. Keep 11px for numerals and single words.
- Set `--layer` on any new surface class.

**Don't**

- Don't put a glow, a gradient or a drop-shadow on anything accent-coloured. The primary button's shadow is neutral.
- Don't draw borders on cards, and don't nest a card in a card.
- Don't make a field a pill or a button a rounded rectangle.
- Don't use a neutral fill for "selected"; that is what hover looks like.
- Don't replace an item's own icon or dot with the check.
- Don't stack two same-coloured bars, or draw a chart as rounded tiles.
- Don't use letter-spaced capitals, a fourth elevation step, a seventh radius or a tenth font size.
- Don't hard-code a hex for a palette, including "just for dark".
- Don't animate layout, shadows or filters, and don't loop anything during a focus block.
- Don't leave navigation reachable behind a modal sheet.
- Don't use the accent in the top bar.

## 10. Not prototyped, and risks

Stated plainly so nobody reads the screenshots as covering more than they do.

- **Glossed and True Minimalist** are specified in section 7 and not drawn. Glass contrast is measured for the worst case; the look is unverified.
- **Motion** beyond hover, press and the switch is a specification.
- **Timer states:** only the rest state is drawn. Running, paused, break, overtime and the finished state reuse the same parts (the arc empties, the phase word and Pomeo's line change, Start becomes Pause) but have not been reviewed as screenshots.
- **22 of 24 palettes** are unmeasured. The two tightest pairs (4.74 text, 3.28 edge) say where they will fail first.
- **Date range, 24-hour time, and the phone sheets for date, time, colour, menu and dialog** follow the rules in 4.22 and are not drawn. Only the preset sheet is.
- **Tablet width** (the slide-in menu) is not drawn.
- **Red-family accents.** Danger is the accent turned to red, so on sunset-like palettes Delete and Start are close in hue. Icon, verb and the one-primary rule carry the difference; a destructive confirm never shows a primary button beside the danger one.
- **Status hues inherit the accent's lightness and chroma.** A very pale or very dark accent gives pale or dark greens and ambers. Text contrast is protected by the 38% text mix; the soft tiles may look weak on a few palettes.
- **The 3:1 edge is the strongest line on a form-heavy screen.** Settings should prefer switches and segmented controls and keep wells for real text, or fifty rows will look wiry.
- **Dark shadows barely show.** In dark the stack is carried by tone and the 1px highlight. Dense screens (leaderboard, clan feed, shop) should be rows inside one card, not a card per item.
- **Cosmetics drawn for v1.20's glossy surfaces** (ring styles including the glow ring, auras, frames, banners, the time-of-day sky, Ripening ring, Focus atmosphere) need a pass on the quiet layers. The calm ring here is the default; a ring style the user equips is their choice, but it should stay one static shadow at most and never a pulse.
- **Layer steps depend on each palette's own `--bg` to `--surface` gap** (1.06–1.09 on the two tested). A palette where they are nearly equal flattens the stack; the top highlight and rim then do the work.
- **The mode dials are a second source of truth** beside the palette: nine numbers per mode. A future high-contrast or true-black mode is a third set of dials, not new colours.
