### Data model (all under users/{uid}, all `v: 1`, all in SYNC_LISTS unless noted)
- Seed shop: no new list — featured and daily seeds are derived from the UTC clock. A daily-seed purchase is an
  ordinary `gbuys` record with `day: 'YYYY-MM-DD'` (UTC day) so the per-day limit is counted from stored data
  on every device. Exclusive species are new `GSPECIES` entries with `shop: 'rotating'`.
- Eggs: `S.eggs` [{ id, v, kind, src, at, w:{sessionId: minutes}, need, h (hatched at), pet, u }] — water maps
  joined like plants (LIST_MERGE), so two devices never lose minutes and a session fills an egg once. The pet is
  decided by `hash01(egg.id)` at hatching and stored. The first egg has a fixed id (`egg1`); the next one is
  `egg` + (hatched + 1), so two devices create the same record. Milestone eggs have fixed ids (`egg_lv4`,
  `egg_lv8`, `egg_ev_<event>`).
- Pets: derived from hatched eggs (`S.eggs` with `pet`), plus `S.petSlots` → `roomPrefs`-style map
  `S.petPrefs` { slot: { v: petId, u } } merged like cardPrefs. A duplicate hatch raises that pet's level (max 3).
- Crop weight: `kg` stored on the plant at harvest (older harvests get the same formula on the fly); records and
  the harvest log are derived; the fold record carries per-species and overall record kg.
- Weather mutations: `wm: ['shocked', …]` stored on the plant when it ripens (the device that waters it decides,
  deterministically from the finishing session and a hash), synced like `m`.
- Garden cosmetics: ordinary `ITEMS` with `gprice` (produce) and no coin price; a purchase is a `gbuys` record
  `{ item: '<item id>', price }` replayed by the produce economy; `computeGame` counts a valid garden purchase as
  ownership, so the bag, equip and every renderer work unchanged.
- Leaderboards: publicStats gets two optional ints, `produce` (lifetime produce earned) and `harvests`
  (lifetime 1.15-garden harvests); publicProfiles gets one optional string `big` ("tomato:4.2") for the friends'
  card. Rank history per board lives in localStorage (this device only).

### Decisions already taken (details in each phase)
- Rotation: Featured = 4 seeds per 4-hour UTC slot (`floor(now / 4h)`), the same for everyone; the Rare seed of
  the day changes at 00:00 UTC (one clock for every device and time zone; shown with a local countdown), limited
  to 2 per user per UTC day. Featured and daily seeds can be bought one level early. Three exclusive species
  (Bamboo, Pineapple, Sakura) appear only there, but the daily seed cycles through every exclusive within 9 days
  and garden levels 6 and 9 gift one each (fixed ids), so nothing is permanently missable.
- Eggs come only from focus (300 minutes for a common egg), garden levels 4 and 8 (rare, legendary) and questline
  finals from the November 2026 questline on (rare). No purchase of any kind.
- Weather mutations: at most two per plant on top of one colour mutation; the total produce multiplier is capped
  at ×8 and weight at ×6.
- Hard-rule compliance is the same shape as 1.15 (profile document only; demo read-only; focus guard kinds
  `reveal` for hatching/records/mutations; Animations gates; True Minimalist hides the garden, pets and the
  cosmetics shop; leaderboards stay plain there).

## Phase 1 — Rotating seed shop (done)

### What was added
- The Seeds view now has three sections. **Featured**: four paid seeds per 4-hour UTC slot (`floor(now / 4h)`,
  hash order, always at least one rare or better), 20% off and buyable one garden level early, with a live
  "Restocks in 1h 44m" countdown (the view re-renders itself when the slot turns). **Rare seed of the day**:
  changes at 00:00 UTC, never common, up to 2 per person per UTC day, one level early, "Changes in 13h 45m";
  every third UTC day it is one of the three exclusive species in turn. **Always available**: the old grid,
  without the exclusives.
- Three exclusive species — Bamboo (rare, 280 min), Pineapple (rare, 330 min), Sakura (legendary, 640 min) —
  sold only in the rotating slots. Nothing is permanently missable: the daily seed cycles through all three
  within 9 days, and garden levels 6, 8 and 9 each gift one (fixed record ids, created once on any device).
- A "Shop restocked" dot on the Garden tab (setting "Dot when the seed shop restocks", on by default): shown when
  the current slot is newer than the last one this device looked at, never during a focus block; the Garden info
  line says the shop has restocked. The ready-to-harvest dot is unchanged.
- Demo: one rare seed of the day already bought (1 left today), Featured shown; buying shows the demo prompt.

### Decisions
- UTC for both rotations (one clock for every device and time zone; the countdown is local time until a UTC
  boundary). Local midnight would have made the same instant a different "day" on two devices of one account.
- A daily purchase stores the UTC day key (`day`) and `src: 'daily'`; the per-day limit is counted from those
  records, so it holds on every device, offline and after a reload — no separate counter to merge. Featured
  purchases store `src: 'feat'` (price recorded as paid); gifts `src: 'gift'`.
- A featured seed that has rotated away between render and tap is refused with a toast and the view refreshes.
- Exclusives are excluded from the "next level unlocks" text and from the plain grid; a plain-grid purchase of
  an exclusive is refused even by script.
