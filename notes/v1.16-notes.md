### Data model (all under users/{uid}, all `v: 1`, all in SYNC_LISTS unless noted)
- Seed shop: no new list — featured and daily seeds are derived from the UTC clock. A daily-seed purchase is an
  ordinary `gbuys` record with `day: 'YYYY-MM-DD'` (UTC day) so the per-day limit is counted from stored data
  on every device. Exclusive species are new `GSPECIES` entries with `shop: 'rotating'`.
- Eggs: `S.eggs` [{ id, v, kind, src, at, w:{sessionId: minutes}, need, h (hatched at), pet, u }] — water maps
  joined like plants (LIST_MERGE), so two devices never lose minutes and a session fills an egg once. The pet is
  decided by `hash01(egg.id)` at hatching and stored. The first egg has a fixed id (`egg1`); the next one is
  `egg` + (hatched + 1), so two devices create the same record. Milestone eggs have fixed ids (`egg_lv4`,
  `egg_lv8`, `egg_ev_<event>`).
- Pets: derived from hatched eggs (`S.eggs` with `pet`), plus `S.petSlots` → `roomPrefs`-style map
  `S.petPrefs` { slot: { v: petId, u } } merged like cardPrefs. A duplicate hatch raises that pet's level (max 3).
- Crop weight: `kg` stored on the plant at harvest (older harvests get the same formula on the fly); records and
  the harvest log are derived; the fold record carries per-species and overall record kg.
- Weather mutations: `wm: ['shocked', …]` stored on the plant when it ripens (the device that waters it decides,
  deterministically from the finishing session and a hash), synced like `m`.
- Garden cosmetics: ordinary `ITEMS` with `gprice` (produce) and no coin price; a purchase is a `gbuys` record
  `{ item: '<item id>', price }` replayed by the produce economy; `computeGame` counts a valid garden purchase as
  ownership, so the bag, equip and every renderer work unchanged.
- Leaderboards: publicStats gets two optional ints, `produce` (lifetime produce earned) and `harvests`
  (lifetime 1.15-garden harvests); publicProfiles gets one optional string `big` ("tomato:4.2") for the friends'
  card. Rank history per board lives in localStorage (this device only).

### Decisions already taken (details in each phase)
- Rotation: Featured = 4 seeds per 4-hour UTC slot (`floor(now / 4h)`), the same for everyone; the Rare seed of
  the day changes at 00:00 UTC (one clock for every device and time zone; shown with a local countdown), limited
  to 2 per user per UTC day. Featured and daily seeds can be bought one level early. Three exclusive species
  (Bamboo, Pineapple, Sakura) appear only there, but the daily seed cycles through every exclusive within 9 days
  and garden levels 6 and 9 gift one each (fixed ids), so nothing is permanently missable.
- Eggs come only from focus (300 minutes for a common egg), garden levels 4 and 8 (rare, legendary) and questline
  finals from the November 2026 questline on (rare). No purchase of any kind.
- Weather mutations: at most two per plant on top of one colour mutation; the total produce multiplier is capped
  at ×8 and weight at ×6.
- Hard-rule compliance is the same shape as 1.15 (profile document only; demo read-only; focus guard kinds
  `reveal` for hatching/records/mutations; Animations gates; True Minimalist hides the garden, pets and the
  cosmetics shop; leaderboards stay plain there).

## Phase 1 — Rotating seed shop (done)

### What was added
- The Seeds view now has three sections. **Featured**: four paid seeds per 4-hour UTC slot (`floor(now / 4h)`,
  hash order, always at least one rare or better), 20% off and buyable one garden level early, with a live
  "Restocks in 1h 44m" countdown (the view re-renders itself when the slot turns). **Rare seed of the day**:
  changes at 00:00 UTC, never common, up to 2 per person per UTC day, one level early, "Changes in 13h 45m";
  every third UTC day it is one of the three exclusive species in turn. **Always available**: the old grid,
  without the exclusives.
- Three exclusive species — Bamboo (rare, 280 min), Pineapple (rare, 330 min), Sakura (legendary, 640 min) —
  sold only in the rotating slots. Nothing is permanently missable: the daily seed cycles through all three
  within 9 days, and garden levels 6, 8 and 9 each gift one (fixed record ids, created once on any device).
- A "Shop restocked" dot on the Garden tab (setting "Dot when the seed shop restocks", on by default): shown when
  the current slot is newer than the last one this device looked at, never during a focus block; the Garden info
  line says the shop has restocked. The ready-to-harvest dot is unchanged.
- Demo: one rare seed of the day already bought (1 left today), Featured shown; buying shows the demo prompt.

### Decisions
- UTC for both rotations (one clock for every device and time zone; the countdown is local time until a UTC
  boundary). Local midnight would have made the same instant a different "day" on two devices of one account.
- A daily purchase stores the UTC day key (`day`) and `src: 'daily'`; the per-day limit is counted from those
  records, so it holds on every device, offline and after a reload — no separate counter to merge. Featured
  purchases store `src: 'feat'` (price recorded as paid); gifts `src: 'gift'`.
- A featured seed that has rotated away between render and tap is refused with a toast and the view refreshes.
- Exclusives are excluded from the "next level unlocks" text and from the plain grid; a plain-grid purchase of
  an exclusive is refused even by script.

  limit and its reset after midnight UTC, gift seeds created once, the restock dot (focus, setting, seen),

## Phase 2 — Garden pets (done)

### What was added
- **Eggs from focus only.** A new garden starts with a common egg in the slot (`egg1`). Every finished focus
  block pours its raw minutes into the egg in the slot (the oldest unhatched one); at 300 minutes (common), 420
  (rare) or 600 (legendary) it hatches, and what is left over flows into the next egg, which is created right
  away (`egg2`, `egg3`, … — the id is derived from the count, so two devices create the same record). Garden level
  4 adds a rare egg and level 8 a legendary one (fixed ids `egg_lv4` / `egg_lv8`), and every questline final from
  the November 2026 questline on adds a rare egg (`egg_ev_<event>`). Nothing is bought.
- **Deterministic hatching.** The pet is decided from the egg's id alone (`hash01(id + ':pet')` picks the tier —
  common eggs 70 / 25 / 5%, rare 20 / 60 / 20%, legendary 0 / 40 / 60% — and a second hash the species), stored on
  the egg, so every device shows the same pet and a reload cannot re-roll it. An egg filled to the brim by two
  devices (water maps joined) hatches on the next check with the same answer.
- **Ten pets** in the existing rarities: Bunny, Hedgehog, Frog, Turtle (common), Bee, Owl, Deer, Fox (rare),
  Hummingbird, Golden snail (legendary), each with a boost: water from sessions (+3…12%), mutation chance
  (+2…2.5%), slower wilting (+1 day), bonus produce (6…10%), crop weight (+5…12%, used from phase 3). A duplicate
  hatch raises the pet's level (hearts, up to 3; +25% boost per heart). **Caps on the total:** water +25%,
  mutation +8%, wilting +2 days, bonus produce 20%, weight +25%.
- **Slots:** 1 pet out at garden level 1, 2 at level 5, 3 at level 8 (`petPrefs` merged slot by slot like the
  room). Pets out sit in the scene (idle bob with Animations on Full; a tap makes them say their line), an egg card
  in the garden head shows the slot's progress and the pets out, the **Pets** dialog shows every pet with hearts,
  boost and Let out / Bring in, plus the egg queue. The bag has a **Pets** category (same cards, same buttons, the
  egg in the slot) and the almanac a Pets section (silhouettes until hatched) with three new badges.
- **Hatch reveal:** a dialog with the egg wobbling and cracking (Animations on Full; instant otherwise) and the pet
  popping out, "Keep it in the bag" / "Let it out" — queued through the focus guard (`reveal`) so it never
  interrupts a block, and shown 5 s after the end-of-block feedback.
- Demo: a bunny out, a fox in the bag, egg2 filling (125 / 300).

### Decisions
- The egg drinks the block's raw minutes; pets' water boost applies to plants only (a pet cannot speed up its own
  successor). Bonus produce is decided from the plant id against the pets out at harvest time and stored in the
  harvest's `y`, so it is the same on every device and never re-rolls.
- Boss wins do not add eggs (the brief said "or"): milestones, garden levels and questline finals do.
- Leftover minutes carry into the next egg rather than being lost, up to four eggs in one block.

### Tests
- `v116b` (17 checks): the first egg, raw-minute fills, replay safety, the distribution of 400 ids and legendary
  eggs never hatching a common pet, the hatch into the predicted pet with the leftover in egg2, the reveal (and
  its hold during a focus block), slots 1 / 2 / 3 by level, duplicates and caps, the applied water (+21% →
  121 min from a 100-min block) and mutation boost, the fox's bonus chance, milestone eggs created once, the
  settle of a two-device egg, the questline cut-off rule, bag and almanac, export/import (and a 1.15 export
  without eggs), demo (no writes), two devices (water joined offline → online, the same pet, petPrefs synced).

## Phase 3 — Crop size and weight (done)

### What was added
- **Every harvest weighs something**, decided only from stored data: a per-species range (`GKG`, e.g. tomato
  120–350 g, pumpkin 3–9 kg, strawberry 20–60 g; species without a range use their rarity's) picked by a hash of
  the plant id, then × up to 1.5 for long watering blocks (average block length 25 → 90 min), × 1.4 / 1.7 / 2 for
  Golden / Rainbow / Crystal, × (1 + pets' weight boost, capped at +25%), × 3 for a **Giant** — everything together
  capped at ×6. The weight is stored on the harvest (`kg`) at harvest time; harvests from before 1.16 get the same
  formula on the fly (same answer on every device), so nothing needed a migration.
- **Giants:** 3 in 100 harvests, 5 in 100 when the plant was grown with hour-long blocks (hash of the plant id, so
  the roll is the same everywhere), stored as `giant: 1`. A Giant harvest opens a reveal dialog with the plant and
  its weight (queued through the focus guard, so it waits for the end of a block) and the harvest toast says GIANT.
- **Records:** the heaviest harvest overall and per species (stored harvests + the folded history, which now keeps
  `kg`, `kgSp`, `kgM`, `kgG` and a per-species `kg`); a "🏆 New record" toast after a harvest that beats one.
- **The harvest log** in the Garden view: the last 12 harvests with species, mutation, weight, produce and date, the
  Giant and the record rows marked, and the "Biggest" line.
- The almanac shows the biggest harvest in its head and the heaviest per species in each species card; two new
  badges: **Giant** (harvest a Giant) and **Heavyweight** (a harvest over 5 kg).
- **The player card** gets a "Biggest harvest" fact (`bh: [species, kg]`, sanitised like every other field: an
  unknown species is dropped) and friends' cards read it from the new optional `big` field of publicProfiles
  (`'species:kg'`). Until the rules accept `big`, the client publishes without it (the same fallback as `aura`).
- Demo: the blueberry harvest is a 2 kg Giant and leads the records.

### Decisions
- The reveal always waits during a focus block (the harvest toast is the block's feedback), the same as a hatch.
- Weight never changes produce, XP, coins or streaks: it is a record and a bragging right only.
- The ×6 cap applies to the multiplier, so a Giant Crystal pumpkin grown with 90-minute blocks weighs at most
  6 × its base range (≤ 54 kg).

### Tests
- `v116c` (13 checks): the formula (deterministic, in range, spread by id, each multiplier, the cap, formatting),
  Giant odds over 20 000 ids (≈3% and ≈5%), stored weights and records across harvests, the harvest + record
  toasts, the Giant reveal (and its hold during a block, after Pomeo's bubble), the harvest log and almanac, the
  player card (`bh`, sanitiser, the `big` field), old harvests weighed on the fly, the fold keeping the records,
  export/import, demo.
- Related suites rerun green: v115a (51), v115f (18), v115h (18), v113a (17), v113b (28, its facts count now allows
  the new card fact), v1143c (6).

## Phase 4 — Focus-weather mutations (done)

### What was added
- **Seven weather mutations**, decided once when a plant ripens, from the block that finished it (and the streak at
  that moment), each by a hash of the plant id — so every device agrees and nothing re-rolls:
  - ⚡ **Shocked** — the ripening block was 90 minutes or longer (40% chance, ×1.5 produce)
  - 🌙 **Moonlit** — the block started between 10 PM and 4 AM (35%, ×1.4)
  - ❄️ **Frozen** — ripened in December (30%, ×1.6)
  - 🛡️ **Clan-blessed** — ripened during a clan live study session (45%, ×1.8)
  - 🌅 **Dawn** — the block started between 4 and 7 AM (35%, ×1.3)
  - 🔥 **Streak-touched** — a streak of 14 days or more (30%, ×1.4)
  - ⛈️ **Stormy** — during a monsoon questline, or a rainy monsoon day (30%, ×1.5)
- **Stacking and caps:** every condition the block meets rolls; the two lowest rolls win (at most two weather
  mutations), on top of one colour mutation (Golden / Rainbow / Crystal). The produce multiplier of everything
  together is capped at ×8; the weight carries the mutations too (×1.1–1.2 each) inside the existing ×6 cap.
- **Looks:** a ripe plant with weather mutations glows in their colours on its plot (Shocked twitches with
  Animations on Full) and wears their icons; the plant dialog is titled "Shocked Golden Tomato" and explains each
  one; the "ready" toast, the harvest toast, the inbox line and the harvest log name them.
- **Almanac:** a chip per weather mutation with its count, a seven-row list (how to get each, the chance, how
  many harvested) with "n of 7", and two badges: **Weatherproof** (any weather mutation) and **Every season**
  (all seven). The counts survive the fold (`fold.wm`).
- Demo: the Golden tomato in the log was also Shocked; the ready basil is Moonlit.

### Decisions
- Time-of-day and month come from the block's start in the user's local time (the same clock the streak uses).
- Clan-blessed reads the session record's `live` field (a block finished while a clan live session was open).
- Stormy uses the five monsoon questlines (Nov 2026, Jun–Sep 2027) or the garden's own rainy monsoon days.
- Plants from before 1.16 have no `wm` and yield exactly what they did (the multiplier for them is unchanged).

### Tests
- `v116d` (15 checks): every condition and its boundary (89 vs 90 minutes, 3:59 vs 4:00, streak 13 vs 14, May vs
  the questline), the at-most-two rule and the "lowest rolls win" pick, the ×8 cap and a 1.15 plant unchanged,
  weight carrying the mutation under ×6, a real 100-minute block ripening a Shocked tomato (plot glow + icon,
  the ready toast, the dialog, replay safety, the harvest pay-out, log, almanac, badge, toast), the fold keeping
  the counts, export/import (and a 1.15 export), two devices seeing the same list, demo, Animations off on a
  390 px phone, True Minimalist hiding the garden.

- **Twenty garden-themed looks sold only in the garden, for produce** (`gprice`), in the existing rarities and gated
  by garden level (common at 1, rare at 3, legendary at 6):
  - profile pictures: Pumpkin, Sunflower (common, 35), Strawberry (rare, 90), Lotus (legendary, 200)

  - ring trails: Falling leaves (rare, 110), Fireflies (legendary, 210)
  - room pieces: Sunflower pot, Seed-packet poster (45 each), Grass rug (rare, 95)
- They are ordinary `ITEMS` with `price: -1` (never for coins; the coin shop lists one only once it is owned, like a
  reward), bought through `gardenBuy` into the garden ledger (`gbuys`, the same records as seeds and decor) and
  **owned through that ledger**: `computeGame` adds every valid look purchase to `owned`, so equipping, the bag

- Prices sit between the seed prices and the legendary decor (common 30–45, rare 85–120, legendary 180–220 produce)

## Phase 6 — Leaderboard v2 and the garden boards (done)

### What was added
- **Two kinds of board:** *Focus time* (Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Lifetime, as before) and *Garden* (**Produce
  earned** and **Plants harvested**, both all-time), for Friends and for Global; a third seg picks the kind and
  remembers the last period of each. The Clans board is unchanged.
- **The look:** a podium for the top three (the leader in the middle with a crown, bigger pictures, their value),
  then rows with rank, a rank arrow, picture, name and value; **your own rank pinned** in a sticky line at the
  bottom ("#4 · You · 313", with your arrow) whether you are in the list or not (the "not in the top 50" pinned row
  is still there as well); Pomeo's empty states (no friends yet / nobody has focused / nobody has harvested).
- **Rank arrows and "You passed …":** where everyone stood the last time each board loaded is kept on the device
  (`pomodorolist-lbprev:<uid>`, the last 16 boards); the next fresh load shows ▲n / ▼n / new per row, and if
  someone who stood above you is now below you, a toast says "You passed @name on the produce board 🎉" (through
  the focus guard like every toast). Showing the same cached board again keeps the arrows and never repeats it.
- **What is published:** two optional `publicStats` fields, `produce` (every produce ever harvested, including
  the migrated summary) and `harvests` (plants harvested ever), whole numbers, published with the minutes and
  clamped the same way: never below the server's value, growing at most 2 produce per minute and a harvest per
  30 minutes since the last write (plus the usual 4-hour allowance), so a big offline catch-up lands over a few
  sessions. `publicProfiles.big` (phase 3) is accepted now. Rules from before 1.16 refuse the two fields: the client
  publishes without them and retries later (the same fallback as `aura`, `clanTag`, `big`).
- Demo: the five demo friends have garden totals (Justin none — an older app — so he is off the garden boards).

### Firestore
- `firestore.rules`: `publicProfiles` gains the optional `big` (`'species:kg'`, `^[a-z]{2,20}:[0-9]{1,4}(\.[0-9]{1,2})?$`,
  ≤ 40 chars); `publicStats` gains the optional `produce` (int, 0–10,000,000) and `harvests` (int, 0–1,000,000)
  with `growsTotal`: a total new on the document may take any value under its ceiling once (an older document, or
  an older app that stopped sending it), after that never down and bounded growth. Older documents and older app
  versions stay valid. The exact diff is in the release notes.
- `firestore.indexes.json`: two more composite indexes on `publicStats` — (`global` ASC, `produce` DESC) and
  (`global` ASC, `harvests` DESC); the rank count query (`global == true`, `produce > n`) uses the same ones.
- **Reads:** a garden board costs what a focus board costs — one query of up to 50 documents plus one count for
  your rank (global), or the friends' cached documents (friends) — and only when opened; the four boards share
  the same 5-minute cache. No new reads on load, sync or idle. Writes: none added (the fields ride the existing
  `publicStats` write).

### Decisions
- The produce board counts produce *earned* (not what is left after spending), so buying seeds never moves you
  down; the harvests board counts every harvest, including the ones credited by the 1.15 migration.
- Arrows compare with the last load of the *same* board in the *same* period; a new day/week/month starts clean.
- Friends still on an older app are left off the garden boards rather than shown with 0.

## Phase 7 — Hardening, review, measurements, full run

### Independent review
A separate reviewer agent read the whole 1.15 → 1.16 diff (`index.html`, the rules, the indexes) with the hard rules of the
brief as its checklist. It confirmed the invariants (no recursion between `computeGame` and `computeGarden`, derived
caches reset on every save, coins / XP / streaks / boss / clan maths untouched, 1.15 exports and profiles still import,
a 1.15 device cannot wipe eggs, no unescaped strings in the new templates, no new reads on load / sync / idle, older
app versions stay valid under the rules) and reported 13 findings — one high, two medium, ten low. All 13 are fixed
(the list is in the release notes under "Bugs found and fixed", items 14–26); the two that mattered most:
- the `publicStats` fallback probe reacted to the original denial instead of the probe write, which against rules
  from before 1.16 would have re-armed a denied write every 300 ms — the probe is now the write itself, and only
  after the normal retry has re-read the server totals;
- `mergePlant` let a newer stale copy drop a ripened plant's `fin` / `fd` / `m` / `wm` — the ripened copy now wins.

### Extra suites
- `v116g` (edge cases): a wipe on a guest (re-migration, fresh first egg, no looks, nothing negative), guest data
  merging into a new account (weighted harvests, mutations, the look, the pet out, both eggs, produce), a 1.14 export
  (sessions only) into 1.16, in-page checks of the plant merge / the egg upload / block lengths / the hatch queue,
  the demo writing nothing from the Seeds view and the boards, two devices buying the same look while one is offline.
- `v116f` gained two fallback checks (a refused write for another reason keeps the fields; simulated old rules drop
  them for the session with no loop); `v1131b` follows this week's boss HP; `sec143` restores the repository's
  current rules when it finishes (it used to put the 1.14.3 rules back, which is what tripped the fallback in the
  first place).

### Measurements (before = 1.15 build `8f09f86`, after = 1.16; scripts `perf116.mjs`, `fsio116.mjs`, `audit116.mjs`)
- **Performance** (guest with 2 years of sessions, 16 growing plants, 1,200 cards; the 1.16 run also carries 400 weighted
  harvests, two eggs, a pet out and a look; medians of 4): desktop load 1,745 ms vs 1,631 ms (+7%); phone (CPU ×4)
  load 4,628 ms vs 4,211 ms (+10%); tab switches within a few ms of each other on desktop, the Garden tab ~40 ms
  slower on the throttled phone (its head now draws the egg card and the harvest log); phone frame rates 50–60 fps on
  the Timer / Stats / Garden tabs with no frame over 50 ms on either build; JS heap 19 MB vs 17 MB (desktop), 10 vs 9
  MB (phone). The desktop frame lines read ~10 fps on both builds: headless Chromium throttles requestAnimationFrame
  in an unfocused 1280-px context, a harness artefact, identical before and after.
- **Firestore usage** (one new user's first day on the emulator, same steps on both builds): 36 reads / 35 writes on
  1.15, 108 reads / 41 writes on 1.16 — the whole difference is the 1.16-only step (72 reads / 6 writes: the looks
  section, the pets dialog, a look bought and the four garden boards opened; each global board reads the top-50
  list plus one count for the rank, as the focus boards always have, cached for 5 minutes). Load, sync, the reload
  (8 reads on both) and a minute idle (0) read nothing new.
- **Layout audit**: 360 / 390 / 768 / 1024 / 1280 / 1440 × light / dark / Glossed / True Minimalist, every tab and
