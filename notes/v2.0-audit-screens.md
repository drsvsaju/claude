# Screen audit before Phase 3 (v2.0)

Nine reviewers looked only at screenshots of the build after Phase 2 (every screen, desktop dark / light and phone) and
listed what most limits each screen. P1 = hurts use or looks broken, P2 = unpolished, P3 = refinement. This is the
worklist for Phase 3; an item is struck through here when it is done or deliberately left.

## 1-timer

Measured on the originals (WCAG, darkest vs lightest pixel): ring track at rest 1.13:1 dark / 1.16:1 light. Quick start chip edge 1.34:1 dark. 'Overtime' label about 4.2:1 light. Rank names in light: Gold 2.96:1, Silver 4.36:1. Rank cell edges in light 1.28–1.92:1. Desktop layout at 1440: content 1008px wide, columns 496/496, timer card top at 196 at rest vs 121 while running.

Cross-screen patterns, fix once:
(1) The per-second digit roll stacks two glyphs in running, break and overtime, in both themes.
(2) Uppercase tracked eyebrow card heads are used on every card and on the rank dialog.
(3) The primary button changes position between states because the side pads differ in width; use fixed slots.
(4) Ring stroke and cap differ in every state: thin track with no arc at rest, a dot plus petal trail while running, a capsule plus halo in break, a thick orange full circle in overtime. One ring component should serve every state.
(5) Dialogs open with the focus-visible ring on a button even after a pointer action.

Coverage gaps: the preset-manager shots show only the demo sign-up gate, so the real preset manager was not reviewed; re-shoot with an editable account. A timer-preset-open shot exists in shots-p3base but was not part of this group.

Crops are in /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-claude/24ffcc98-6b94-5cf9-9f38-83a211e86577/scratchpad/crops-1timer/ (for example c3-timer-card-dark.jpg, c5-today-dark.jpg, c9-running-card-dark.jpg, c10-overtime-light.jpg, c19-rank-dark.jpg, c22-phone-bottoms.jpg).

### timer

**Main thing:** The hero is not a hero: the ring at rest is an invisible grey track with no arc, and the Today card beside it is an overloaded wall of nested boxes, bars and inline links. Rebuild both per sections 4.23 and 5 before anything else.

- **P1** (all) *Timer card, ring at rest (25:00)* — No accent arc, no ticks and no cap at rest. Only a track remains, at 1.13:1 in dark and 1.16:1 in light, so the one element students stare at reads as an empty, disabled dial and almost disappears on cheap laptop panels. Ring is about 292px instead of 320. **Fix:** Follow 4.23: a 320px ring (240 on phone) with a full 4px --accent-fill arc at rest that empties clockwise, a 4px --track of the same width, 12 five-minute ticks inside the arc (plus the 60 minute ticks on desktop), and the 18px leaf cap at the arc head.
- **P1** (all) *Today card (right column, top)* — Overloaded and flat. Two identical '23 days' numbers sit side by side (current and longest streak). Two boxes are nested inside the card: an orange-tinted milestone box and a Platinum III box. Three progress bars in three colours compete. The daily goal is an unlabelled '1h 15m of 2h'. Five 13px sentences follow, holding five accent or orange inline links. On phone those lines get irregular 20–35px gaps, and 'Plan: Chemistry is 49m behind ·' leaves a dangling dot with 'Focus on Chemistry' orphaned on the next line. Mid-study, a student cannot find 'how much is left today'. **Fix:** Rebuild as section 5 says. Two key numbers at 24/600 split by a divider: streak with the caption 'best 23', and focused today '1h 15m of 2h'. Then one goal bar with 'Daily goal · 2 sessions so far · 63%'. Then 44px ledger rows (icon 16, label ink-2, value 600, chevron): Next reminder, To-dos (with a warning icon on '1 overdue'), Weekly plan, JEE Main (absorbing the separate exam card), Clan · Live. Move the milestone and rank boxes out (to Profile or a streak popover). Padding 20/20/8.
- **P2** (all) *Page grid, greeting and Monsoon Study banner* — The columns are 1:1 (496 + 496 inside a 1008px content area at 1440), so the timer card has the same width and fill as Today and does not lead. The greeting is a 14px grey caption, so the page has no title. The Monsoon Study banner is a full-width card with Open and close buttons that pushes the timer 75px down. On phone it pushes Start to about y 618 and truncates to '15 days left · 4 / 8 quests …', hiding 'rewards ready', the only actionable part. **Fix:** Use a 1.6fr / 1fr grid, 20px gap, 1140px max width. Make the timer .card--hero (radius 22, padding 24, static 4–6% accent wash) and leave Today, garden and tip as .card--quiet. Set the greeting as a 22/600 title. Replace the banner with one line of text at the right of the greeting ('Monsoon Study · 15 days left · 4 / 8 quests · rewards ready →'). On phone, put that line under the greeting and let it wrap.
- **P2** (all) *Timer numerals* — '25:00' is set in a light weight (about 300) at about 72px. Thin strokes break up on low-DPI panels and over the glass wash, and the hero has less presence than the bold card titles around it. **Fix:** Set 76/500 with tabular figures and −0.025em tracking (60/500 on phone), in --ink.
- **P2** (all) *Card header: 'Focus' label and round dots* — The phase word and four 6px hollow round dots sit in the card header, outside the ring, squeezed between Pomeo and the sound button. Current and upcoming rounds differ only by colour, the three upcoming dots are about 1.3:1 in dark (state by colour alone, and nearly invisible), and nothing says 'Round 1 of 4'. Pomeo is a mute sticker with no line. **Fix:** Inside the ring, top to bottom: phase (13/600 accent-ink), time, preset name (14 ink-2), then 8px dots (done filled, upcoming a 1.5px ring) followed by 'Round 1 of 4' (12 ink-2). The header keeps only Pomeo with a one-line bubble ('Ready when you are.') and the icon pads.
- **P2** (all) *Controls row and Quick start* — Reset, Start and Skip are all about 51px tall, text-only pills, so the primary differs from its neighbours only by fill. Reset is live at 25:00, when there is nothing to reset. In dark, the Quick start chip edges measure 1.34:1, so the chips read as loose text (below the 3:1 edge rule). **Fix:** Start: a 52px primary, at least 184px wide, with a play icon. Reset and Skip: 44px pads with icon plus word, inside fixed equal-width side slots so the primary never moves between states. Reset is disabled at rest. Quick start chips get the --edge stroke (3:1 or better), with 'Quick start' inline at their left.
- **P2** (all) *Session card* — The head is an uppercase tracked 'SESSION' eyebrow with no meta. The same eyebrow style is used for TODAY, YOUR GARDEN, TIP OF THE DAY and RECENTLY USED, against the 14/600 sentence-case rule. The fields form a single stack: an accent '+ Add subtasks' link is wedged between the task field and Preset, Tag is a bare text field with '(optional)' in its label, and the colour well has no label. **Fix:** Head: 'Session' with right meta '4 rounds · ends about 12:05 PM'. Row 1: 'What are you working on?' plus a labelled Colour well. Row 2, side by side: a Subject / tags chip field (meta '2 of 5') and the Preset select showing '25 / 5 ×4' inline. Put 'Add subtasks' as a quiet button at the bottom left, with the Space / S key hints on the right. Convert every card head on the screen to sentence case.
- **P2** (all) *Your garden card* — The plant glyphs have no names or percentages. The ready plant is marked only by a coral bar, so the cue is colour alone, and coral reads as wilting or error. 'Basil is ready to harvest 🧺' offers no action. The phone shows only 6 of the 7 tiles. **Fix:** Follow the garden glance spec: 7 tiles in one row, each with a 36px glyph, a 3px growth bar, the name at 12/500 and a percentage or 'Ready'. The ready tile gets an accent edge, a tint and a corner check. Footer: a 'Ready' chip, 'Basil is ready to harvest' and one small neutral Harvest pad.
- **P2** (all) *Recently used card* — This is a third preset picker on one screen, alongside Quick start and the Preset select. Each chip repeats itself ('Deep Work 50/10 · 50/10') and nests a star toggle of about 20px inside the pill: a button inside a button, well under 44px on phone. 'Clear' is an accent link with no confirmation. **Fix:** Remove the card. Put 'Recent' and 'Starred' groups at the top of the Preset select's list, with the star as a full-height row action. Feed the Quick start chips from the starred presets.
- **P3** (all) *Tip of the day card* — It uses a 💡 colour emoji and an uppercase eyebrow, not Pomeo. It is one of the four places the spec gives Pomeo, so the screen loses its mascot moment. **Fix:** Put Pomeo (32px) at the left, the title 'Pomeo's tip of the day' at 14/600 and the tip as body text. Make it a content-height quiet card.

### timer-running

**Main thing:** The running state looks broken: the seconds digit renders as two glyphs stacked on top of each other, and on phone the dimmed tab bar lets the Session card show through it. Fix the focus-mode treatment and the per-second update.

- **P1** (all) *Timer numerals, last digit* — The seconds digit rolls with the old and new glyphs overlaid at full opacity ('24:58' with the 8 and 9 stacked; the same in break and overtime). The overlapped digit also looks heavier than its neighbours. Every glance mid-block shows a garbled time, and the animation loops for the whole block, against 'nothing loops while the timer runs except the arc'. **Fix:** Remove the digit roll. Swap the text in place with tabular figures. If any transition is kept, use a 120ms opacity fade where the old glyph is gone before the new one appears; switch it off entirely under Reduced and Off.
- **P1** (phone) *Tab bar during a focus block* — Focus dimming is applied as opacity to the tab bar, so the Session card's Preset select ('Classic 25/5' and its pencil) shows through and collides with the Timer / Reminders / More icons and labels. **Fix:** Dim with a scrim layer placed under the hero card and over the page content. The tab bar keeps its opaque --frame fill (dimmed icons at most), and nothing behind it may bleed through.
- **P2** (all) *Dimmed sidebar, top bar, Today and Session* — Everything except the timer drops to about 35% opacity but stays clickable. Sidebar labels and Today text fall to about 2:1, so they are unreadable yet live targets. The task name in the Session card, the one piece of context a student wants while working, is dimmed with the rest. **Fix:** Dim non-hero content to about 60% and restore it on hover or :focus-within, or move it behind a scrim that a click dismisses. Show the task name ('Physics – Rotational Motion') under the time on the hero while a block runs.
- **P2** (all) *Whole layout on Start* — Pressing Start reflows the page. The Monsoon banner and the Quick start row disappear, the hero shrinks about 45px, the Session card jumps about 120px, and the primary button shifts 24px left because 'Finish early' is wider than 'Skip'. The button the student just pressed moves away from their pointer. **Fix:** Keep the hero's height constant across states: reserve the Quick start row's height and fill it while running with 'Round 1 of 4 · ends about 12:05'. Use fixed equal-width side slots so Pause sits exactly where Start was. The banner goes away for good (see the timer screen).
- **P2** (all) *Controls row: Reset next to Pause* — Reset is an equal-weight pad directly beside Pause during a running block. One mis-tap discards up to 25 minutes of focus without confirmation. **Fix:** While running, turn Reset into a quiet icon-only button with an undo toast ('Block reset · Undo'), or ask for confirmation once more than a minute has elapsed. Show Finish early as an icon-plus-word pad in the right slot.
- **P2** (all) *Header 'P' button* — The control is the colour 🅿️ emoji inside a pad: the only emoji in a line-icon system, blue on teal, with no visible meaning (a parking lot for distractions?). It also only appears once the block runs, which shifts the header. **Fix:** Use an 18px line icon (a notepad with a plus) in the same pad as the sound button, a tooltip and aria-label ('Park a thought'), and show it in every timer state.
- **P2** (all) *Ring during focus* — At 24:59 the ring shows no arc, because it fills from empty. Under the cap dot, a trail of falling petals animates and reads as grey smudges on dark. That is a looping decoration during a focus block, and progress is invisible for the first minutes. **Fix:** Draw a full arc that empties clockwise (same as timer). Keep the leaf cap only, and show no particle or trail cosmetic while a block runs (play it once on completion, if at all).

### timer-overtime

**Main thing:** Overtime switches to a thick orange alarm ring with orange numerals and puts a settings link in the control zone. Keep the calm ring and make the two end actions unambiguous.

- **P2** (all) *Ring and numerals* — The ring becomes a full circle in orange about 8px thick, twice the focus stroke, and the 72px numerals turn orange too. The state reads as an alarm. A full-size '+' glyph makes '+03:04' span about 93% of the ring's inner width, so in light it nearly touches the stroke. **Fix:** Keep a 4px stroke on the same track, in a --warn-fill tone. Keep the numerals in --ink. Show the plus as a smaller prefix at about 60% size, or render 'Overtime' plus '3:04' with the phase word carrying the state.
- **P2** (d-light) *'Overtime' phase label* — The orange label measures about 4.2:1 on the white hero card, below the 4.5:1 text minimum. **Fix:** Use a --warn-ink token measured at 4.5:1 or better on the hero in every palette, and add the 'Overtime' word plus a small clock-plus icon so the state is not carried by colour alone.
- **P2** (all) *Explanation text and 'Turn off overtime' link* — A two-line centred paragraph ends in the widow 'block)'. An accent 'Turn off overtime' link sits about 8px above Stop, putting a settings change in the primary tap zone, where a stray tap disables the feature mid-session. **Fix:** Put the message in Pomeo's one-line bubble: 'Block done. Extra minutes still count (up to 4 h).' Move 'Turn off overtime' into the sound/settings menu, or to a quiet link under the round line at least 24px from the pads.
- **P2** (all) *Controls: Pause / Stop / Break* — 'Stop' and 'Break' both end overtime, and the labels do not say which one saves the extra minutes. Pausing overtime is also an odd choice to offer as a peer. **Fix:** Primary: 'Finish · save 28 min' (or 'Stop & save'). Secondary: 'Start break' with an icon. Drop Pause in overtime, or make it icon-only. Keep the side slots fixed (same as timer-running).
- **P3** (all) *Round dots* — Round 1 still shows as a hollow ring although the block is done. In break the same dot is filled, so the dots contradict the 'Block done' message. **Fix:** Fill the round's dot when its focus time completes (on entering overtime), and show 'Round 1 of 4 · done' inside the ring.

### timer-break

**Main thing:** The break has a glowing ring of a third weight and otherwise looks identical to the rest state. Give it the calm shared ring and a clear 'step away' identity.

- **P2** (all) *Break ring* — A pale teal halo surrounds a thicker grey track (about 10px) with a capsule-shaped cap. That makes a third stroke weight and a third cap shape across rest, focus and break, and the halo is a glow the system forbids. **Fix:** Use the same 4px arc and track as focus, in a calm break tone that empties clockwise, with the leaf cap, no halo and no outer ring.
- **P2** (all) *Break state as a whole* — The break is indistinguishable from the idle screen: the page is undimmed, the Monsoon banner returns, and the phase word 'Short break' is in grey ink-2, the weakest cue of any state. Nothing prompts the student to step away or shows what comes next. **Fix:** Phase word in a break ink. Pomeo's bubble: 'Stand up, water, eyes off the screen.' Under the time: 'Next: Focus · Round 2 of 4'. Keep the page calm and the banner gone (same as timer). Offer break flashcards as one quiet pad, if enabled.
- **P2** (all) *Controls: Reset / Pause / Skip* — 'Skip' does not say what it skips, and Reset is meaningless during a break. Pause is a filled primary for a phase the student should simply let run. **Fix:** Right slot: 'Skip break' with an icon (it starts focus). Hide Reset during breaks. Keep Pause as the primary, but in the fixed slot (same as timer-running). The digit roll is the same as timer-running (visible as 04:58/9 in dark).

### preset-manager

**Main thing:** The preset manager never appears in the demo: the edit pencil leads to a sign-up gate, so the screen cannot be reviewed and demo visitors never see presets. Open it read-only and gate only Save.

- **P1** (all) *Preset edit (pencil) → 'Make it yours' dialog* — Every shot shows the demo sign-up gate, not the preset manager. A student trying the demo hits a dead end on the app's core customisation, and Phase 3 has no picture of this screen to redesign against. **Fix:** In the demo, open the preset manager read-only: the full preset list and fields, inputs disabled, and a one-line notice bar ('Demo · changes aren't saved' plus 'Create free account'). Gate only Save. Re-shoot this screen with an editable account before redesigning it.
- **P2** (phone) *Gate dialog* — The dialog opens as a centred floating card with stacked buttons of unequal width ('Create free account' about 186px, 'Keep exploring' about 136px), not the bottom sheet the spec requires for phone dialogs. **Fix:** Use a bottom sheet: pop layer, 22px top corners, handle, a 44px head with a close button, and a footer of two equal 44px pads side by side.
- **P2** (all) *'Create free account' button* — The dialog opens with the focus-visible double ring on the primary, so the only accent fill wears a halo that reads as glow on a pointer-opened dialog. On desktop the two pads also differ in width. **Fix:** When the dialog opens from a pointer, focus the dialog container or title (tabindex -1) so the ring shows only for keyboard users. Give both footer pads equal width.
- **P3** (all) *Gate copy* — The body opens with the restriction ('nothing can be changed'), which frames the demo as broken, not as a preview. **Fix:** Lead with the benefit: 'Save your own presets, sessions and settings with a free account. The demo resets when you leave.'

### rank-ladder

**Main thing:** The student's own rank is a 10px corner label inside a read-only grid built from choose-from wells, and on phone the dialog opens with its title cut off. Lead with the student's position and render the ladder as a table.

- **P1** (phone) *Dialog top* — The dialog is taller than the viewport and opens with its title ('RANKS · NOV 2026 SEASON') clipped at the top edge. The only way out is the footer, and the student loses the context of what they opened. **Fix:** Use a bottom sheet capped at 85% height, with a sticky head (title 17/600 plus a 44px close) and a sticky footer, and the ladder scrolling inside.
- **P2** (all) *Division cells* — Every division is an 8px-radius bordered well, the shape reserved for things you type in or choose from, but the content is read-only, so it invites clicks. Bronze, Silver and Gold have coloured edges, while Diamond, Master and Grandmaster edges almost vanish (1.28:1 in light), so the table looks half-built. **Fix:** Render it as a ladder table: one 44–48px row per rank with a divider above, and three plain cells (numeral at 12/600, then hours) with no borders. Highlight only the student's row.
- **P2** (all) *The student's position ('You')* — The answer the student came for is a 10px accent 'You' in a cell corner plus a 2px bar about 10% filled, repeated in a small grey sentence at the bottom. **Fix:** Open with a summary block: 'Platinum III' with the 24/600 key number '51 h this season' and one bar captioned '9 h to Platinum II'. In the table, give the student's cell an accent-soft fill, a 1.5px accent-ink ring and a 'You' label chip. Show 'next' with a word as well as the dashed edge.
- **P2** (d-light) *Rank names* — The Gold name measures 2.96:1 and Silver 4.36:1 on the white dialog, and rank identity rides on the text colour. **Fix:** Set rank names in --ink at 600 and keep the rank colour only in the gem icon, at 3:1 or better against the pop layer.
- **P2** (all) *Title and footer* — The dialog title is a 12px uppercase tracked eyebrow. The filled accent goes to 'Close' (dismissal), while the navigational 'Ranks board' is the outlined pad and opens wearing the focus double ring. **Fix:** Title: 'Ranks' at 17/600 with the meta 'November season'. Put a close X in the head. Make 'Ranks board →' the single pad, or a neutral pad with Close quiet. Focus the container on open (same as preset-manager).
- **P3** (all) *Hour ranges and order* — The ranges are truncated decimals (3.3–6.6, 33.3–41.6, 93.3–106.6 h, where 41.6 should be 41.7). The ladder runs from Bronze at the top to Grandmaster at the bottom, so climbing reads downward. Master and Grandmaster repeat their name inside their only cell. **Fix:** Show hours as whole numbers or 'h m' ('3 h 20 m'). Put the highest rank at the top, or add an 'up' cue. Master and Grandmaster rows show just the hours in one spanning cell.

## 2-welcome

Problems shared across the group, for the redesign to fix once: (1) The one-filled-accent rule is broken in every state. The hero's 'Preview the app' stays filled even with the auth card open, and the closing CTA reverses the hero's choice. Decide the page primary once. (2) 'Free / no ads / no tracking' is repeated five times (subtitle, chips, auth intro, table rows, FAQ). Spend the subtitle on what the app does instead. (3) Pills are used as labels: 11 non-pressable pill chips with emoji icons should become plain text with SVG icons, or be removed. (4) Cards are nested: feature-card illustration panels and the session rows. (5) Letter-spaced capitals appear in the comparison table and the tutorial eyebrow. (6) The colour wash ends in a hard edge at one viewport height in the full-page captures, cutting the phone mock card. Confirm with a real scroll before fixing. (7) The auth card changes the hero's layout: the page jumps about 75 px on desktop and the sheet top moves about 190 px between tabs on phone. Fix the layout so the card never drives the hero's position. (8) Phone targets under 44 px: header Log in, swatches, look segments, auth segmented control and close. Measurements are in CSS px from the 2× originals. Contrast figures (Minimalist meta about 2.7:1, tutorial dots about 1.05:1, translucent primary about 4.4:1 on its right half) are pixel samples from the JPEGs and are approximate. ### welcome

**Main thing:** The landing page has no single path: three ways in with mixed weights, the same 'free, no ads, no tracking' line said five times, and a hero that never shows what makes the app different (garden, Pomeo, bosses, friends).

- **P1** (all) *Hero CTA block: 'Preview the app' (filled), 'Create free account' (pad), 'Continue without an account' (text link), the two-line footnote, and the closing 'Ready to focus?' CTA* — There are three entry paths and the student has to read a footnote to choose between them. The hero makes Preview the filled button, while the subtitle sells 'Create a free account' and the closing CTA swaps the weights (filled 'Create a free account', pad 'Preview the app'). The labels also differ ('Create free account' vs 'Create a free account'). The text link starts about 5 px right of the column edge. **Fix:** Pick one primary for the whole page and use the same label in the hero and the closing CTA (for example a filled 'Create free account' plus a 'Preview the app' pad). Remove the 'Continue without an account' link and the footnote from the hero, because the sign-up sheet already offers that path with its explanation. If Preview needs a note, put one 12px --ink-2 line under its button. Align every hero element to the same left edge.
- **P1** (phone) *Background colour wash behind the hero; the hard edge falls at one viewport height (about 844 px on phone, 900 px on desktop)* — The wash stops in a hard horizontal line. On the phone that line cuts the hero mock card in half: above it the card is lit teal-grey, below it the card is flat black. It looks like a rendering bug at the moment the page tries to impress. If the layer is position:fixed this may only be a capture artefact, so confirm with a real scroll on an Android phone before fixing. If a student can see it, it stays P1. **Fix:** Fade the wash to --bg over its last 30–40% with a mask-image or gradient, or make it a fixed full-page layer behind every section so no edge exists. Never end it inside a card.
- **P2** (all) *Hero subtitle 'Create a free account: no ads, no tracking, no subscription.' and the three check chips under it* — The subtitle and the chips say the same thing back to back. The same point comes again in the sign-up card, in two comparison-table rows and in the FAQ. Nothing above the fold tells a 16–18-year-old what the app does for exam study, so the selling space goes on reassurance instead of value. **Fix:** Rewrite the subtitle as the value proposition in one sentence of at most 20 words, e.g. 'A Pomodoro timer for exam season: grow a garden, beat a weekly boss and study with friends.' Keep 'Free forever · No ads · No tracking' once, as the chip row or a single 13px line.
- **P2** (all) *The three check chips ('Free forever', 'No ads', 'No tracking') and the eight emoji chips under the mock (Focus timer … Reminders)* — These 11 pill-shaped pads do nothing when pressed, which breaks the rule that a pill is something you press. The emoji icons render differently on Android, Windows and Mac and don't match the 1.75-stroke SVG icon set. The eight feature chips repeat the feature grid one scroll below. They also name 'Clans' and 'Streak rewards', while the grid has no Clans card and calls the second one 'Streak milestones'. **Fix:** Remove the eight feature chips; the grid already covers them. Render the three promises as plain text with a 16px SVG check in --accent-ink, with no fill and no pill. If any chip stays, it must be a link that scrolls to its grid card and use the SVG icons.
- **P2** (d-dark) *Hero right column: 'Pomodorolist' mock window (about 300×155 px) with traffic-light dots* — The mock is small in a 1067 px hero and floats in empty space. Its macOS window dots suggest a desktop app (students may look for an install). It shows a generic ring and bars rather than the garden, Pomeo or the boss, which set the app apart from every other timer. The left column is heavy and the right column is sparse. **Fix:** Grow the visual to fill the column (about 480–520 px wide), drop the traffic lights, and compose it from the real app: timer card with Pomeo's speech bubble, the garden row with a ready plant, and the weekly boss bar. Align its top with the headline's cap height.
- **P2** (all) *Feature grid 'Everything you need to study and work in focused blocks'* — Eight cards in three columns leave an empty cell at the bottom right, so the grid looks unfinished. Each card holds a bordered illustration panel, which is a card inside a card; 'Study sessions' nests three levels deep (card, panel, row cards). The design system forbids nesting cards. **Fix:** Add the missing Clans card to make nine cards (3×3), or use a 4×2 grid at 1180 px and wider. Replace the inner panels with a borderless illustration area on a --hover tint, radius 12, no edge, and draw the session rows without their own card edges.
- **P2** (d-dark) *'True Minimalist mode' card illustration* — The '25:00' numerals are drawn twice, offset and overlapping, so in a still frame it reads as a glitch. 'Lv 12 · +25 XP' and '23 days' measure about 2.7:1 against the panel, below the 4.5:1 minimum. It shows up on phone and desktop alike. **Fix:** Show a single static state, or two side-by-side states labelled 'Full' and 'Minimal'. If it animates, the resting frame must be clean and the animation must respect reduced motion. Raise the meta text to --ink-2.
- **P2** (phone) *'Free, not "free trial"' comparison table* — On a 390 px phone the headers 'POMODOROLIST' and 'TYPICAL PAID APPS' run into each other with no gap. Row labels wrap to three lines and 'Often a paid extra' wraps to two, so the table becomes a dense stack. The headers use letter-spaced capitals, which the system forbids. Seven 'Included' values in accent ink add up to a column of colour noise. **Fix:** Under 600 px, turn the table into a ledger list: label on the left, a check icon plus 'Free' in --ink 600 on the right, and the competitor note as a 12px --ink-2 caption under the label. On desktop, set the headers in sentence case 12/600 --ink-2 and make 'Included' --ink with only the check in --accent-ink.
- **P2** (all) *Section widths: hero (186–1253 px, 1067 wide), feature grid (974 wide), comparison table and FAQ (720 wide); FAQ drawn as 5 separate cards* — Each section has its own width and left edge, so the page lacks a spine and reads as stitched together. ## 4a-focus-tools

Problems that cut across the group. Fix each once in the shared parts, not per screen.
1. Letter-spaced capital headings appear everywhere: REVISION, CLAN STUDY SESSIONS, TODAY, NEW REMINDER, NEW TASK, QUESTION, EXAM COUNTDOWN, SUBTASK TEMPLATES, WEEKLY PLAN. The design direction forbids them. Card heads become 14/600 sentence case and dialog titles 17/600.
2. Dialogs disagree on how to leave. Only flashcard-review has a close ×. Reminder-form and plan put Cancel and Save on the right, todo-form puts them on the left, and exams and templates have only Done. One head (title plus 32 px close) and one foot (hint on the left, Cancel plus primary on the right) should serve all six dialogs.
3. On the phone, all six dialogs open as centred floating cards with the tab bar behind the scrim, not as the 4.22 full-height bottom sheet with a sticky foot.
4. Focus on open draws the full keyboard ring. It lands on whatever comes first, which is Delete in exams and the close button in flashcard-review, and the double teal ring reads as a glow. Focus should land on the first field or the primary, and the outer ring should show only for :focus-visible.
5. The one-filled-accent rule is broken on reminders (3 fills), todo (2, both for the same 'add' action) and flashcards (4).
6. Subjects appear as bare coloured text (Physics 4.0:1, Chemistry 3.6:1, JEE 3.2:1 in light mode), clash with the amber warn colour, and are typed as free text in todo-form and plan. One shared subject chip and one subject picker would fix reminders, todo, flashcards, todo-form and plan together.

8. The hub's page title stays 'Reminders' on all three tabs.
The sidebar's anonymous red dots, which appear on every desktop shot, belong to the shell review and are not counted here.
