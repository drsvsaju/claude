## Plan (short)
- **Phase 1 — bug fixes.**
  1. Watering. Suspected root causes to reproduce first: (a) a block that ends before the garden is ready on this
     device (fresh device / cleared cache: `gardenMig()` is null until the profile loads, `gardenAfterSession` returns
     null and the block is lost for good — nothing replays it); (b) a plant planted on another device that has not
     synced here yet when the block ends (it has no `w[s.id]` and nothing gives it the block later); (c) the "water
     first" plant swallowing the whole block when its room ≥ the block, so the others get nothing (felt as "didn't
     water my marigold"); (d) blocks under a minute. Fix: a small synced garden queue of recent blocks (`S.gq`)
     that is replayed idempotently whenever the garden becomes ready or a merge brings new plants — plants that got
     nothing from a block are marked with an explicit 0 so a replay never double-waters; the focused plant gets a
     bonus share instead of first pick.
  2. Streak: a goal history (`S.goalLog`: from-day + goal) so a past day is judged against the goal in effect that
     day; a day whose goal bonus was claimed counts as met whatever the goal is now; today against the current goal.
  3. Animation audit: ticker / ring / bars / flame re-renders, conflicting transitions, innerHTML rewrites that
     restart CSS animations.
  4. Challenges: coins for at most one challenge per preset per week, from the 1.17 release week on (older claims
     keep their coins so nothing already bought is invalidated); joining stays possible, the card says so.
  5. Scholar (a card style, `card_scholar`, not an app theme): the free banners get style-aware tints so they sit
     with every card style's palette, plus matching starting banners.
- **Phase 2** — logo (SVG tomato + wordmark; sidebar / tablet menu / phone header; click → Timer), Finish early
  (`finishPhase('early')`: logs the focused minutes as a shortened session with `early: 1`, minimum 5 min, counts
  proportionally everywhere because every system reads `duration`), quick garden card on the Timer tab.
- **Phase 3** — plant XP (water → XP, bars), share formula `raw × n^-0.5` per plant (documented), coin boosts
  (1.5× / 2×, by sessions, capped), Harvests inventory (harvested plants stay as items until sold; migration marks
  existing harvests sold), stars, garden tutorial, extra features (daily garden tasks, compost, trophy shelf, plant
  info card, scene life, harvest pop, harvest festival, backgrounds by level — final list in the phase notes).
- **Phase 4** — 3 pet slots, buffs (old → new table), eggs filled in parallel, pet mutations (deterministic).
- **Phase 5** — daily rewards cycling past day 7 with a weekly rhythm and a capped long-streak bonus; polish.
- **Phase 6** — suites per phase, reviewer agent, perf + Firestore before/after, layout audit, full run.

Decisions and findings are recorded per phase below.
